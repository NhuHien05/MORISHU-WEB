ABOUT MORISHU

Bringing the essence

A premium fruit-juice brand from Vietnam, built on a simple belief: everyday moments are better with real fruit.

OUR STORY

Our story

MORISHU began with a simple observation: Vietnam grows some of the most flavorful fruit in the world, yet most juice on the shelf tastes like it came from anywhere else.

We set out to change that — combining the abundance of Vietnam's tropical fruit, modern production technology, and international quality standards to deliver juice that tastes genuinely of the fruit it's named after.

MISSION & VISION

Our mission & vision

Mission

Deliver high-quality, fruit-based beverages that bring freshness and genuine enjoyment to people, every day.

Vision

Become a trusted, recognized fruit-beverage brand in the global market, known for authenticity and quality.
